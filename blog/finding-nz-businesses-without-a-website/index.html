How We Find Local NZ Businesses Without a Website (And Offer Help Respectfully)

Across New Zealand, plenty of reliable local trades, clinics and service businesses operate without a website. Some rely on a social page that can be hard to find on Google. At PBIwise we help small businesses with digital tools, dashboards and websites. We start by asking where a website would actually make a customer’s life easier.

Spotting local businesses with no website

We pick one trade and town and look through the results from a free local business finder. A blank website field gives us a lead to check. Before contacting anyone, we search for the business by name and check its social profile for a website under a different name.

Sending a personal, helpful offer

We use Smart Email Outreach to send a small number of introductions from our own mailbox. Each note mentions the business, its town and one thing a customer could do more easily online. For a bricklayer, that might be seeing examples of past work and sending a photo with a quote request.

When an owner replies, we review the suggested answer ourselves. The owner knows which enquiries are worth their time and may already have as much work as they want.
