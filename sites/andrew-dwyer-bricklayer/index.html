About Andrew Dwyer

30 Years of Experience You Can Rely On

Based on Brydon Street in Renwick, Andrew Dwyer Bricklayer has been operating across the Marlborough region for three decades. Over the years, we have worked alongside local homeowners, rural properties, and builders on everything from full new-home brickwork to intricate repairs.

Good masonry is about preparation, clean mortar lines, and doing the unseen structural work properly. Whether it is repairing earthquake-damaged brickwork, building a block fence, or crafting a feature stone wall or fireplace, you deal directly with an experienced local tradesman from quote to clean-up.
