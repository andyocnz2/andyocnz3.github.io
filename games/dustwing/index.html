Dustwing

Arcade air combat for iPhone and iPad

Dustwing is fast arcade air combat. Pick a jet or helicopter, choose a top-down 2.5D view or full 3D chase and cockpit views, and fight Blue vs Red against bots across four maps: Desert, Red Canyon, Islands and Alpine.

  • Play friends on the same Wi-Fi with Host / Join, no internet or account needed
  • Touch controls, optional tilt-to-fly, auto-landing and auto pull-up keep it easy to pick up
  • No ads, no tracking, no data collection
