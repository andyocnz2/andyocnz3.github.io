Overrun Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · OCNZ Venture Limited

Summary

Overrun does not collect, store or share personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your callsign and game settings (weapon, view, zoom, volume, music) are saved locally on your device so they are remembered next time. They never leave the device unless you play multiplayer.

Local Wi-Fi multiplayer

When you choose Host or Join on Wi-Fi, the app uses your local network (Bonjour) to find and connect to nearby players. Your callsign and in-game actions are sent directly between devices on that network for the match only. Nothing is sent to us or stored on any server.

Children

Overrun does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
