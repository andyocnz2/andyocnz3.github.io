Paper Tanks Support

Two-player ink and fold tank battle for iPhone and iPhone Duo

Contact

Email info@pbiwise.com with your device model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

How to play

  • Tap PLAY. The page splits at the fold: RED on one half, BLUE on the other.
  • On your turn, draw an ink blob on your own half. The ink meter shows how much ink you have left; tap Clear to start again.
  • Fold: on iPhone Duo, close the phone and open it again. On other iPhones, tap FOLD!
  • The ink prints in the mirror spot on the other half. Any tank it touches is destroyed.
  • Destroy all 3 enemy tanks to win.

Common fixes

  • Nothing happens when I draw: start the stroke on your own half, the one marked YOUR TURN.
  • FOLD! is greyed out: draw some ink first.
  • On iPhone Duo, open the phone fully to see the result after a fold.
