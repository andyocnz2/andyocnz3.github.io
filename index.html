Project Details

Client:
Innovative Architecture Analytics with Power BI 3D Visualization
Services:
Development of a comprehensive Power BI dashboard integrating with Autodesk Revit for detailed area and room analysis by floor, complemented with interactive 3D modeling capabilities.

Description

An architecture firm required advanced analytical capabilities to evaluate area distribution and room specifics within their AutoCAD Revit designs. The traditional approach was cumbersome and disconnected from real-time data. PBIWise addressed this challenge by creating a Power BI solution that seamlessly ingests Revit file data. Our dashboard provides layered insights into area usage by floor and analysis by individual rooms, enriched with interactive 3D models within Power BI. This integration elevates design decision-making, ensuring adherence to project specifications and optimizing spatial efficiency for the client’s projects.

Questions, answered.

What happens in the AI Opportunity Assessment?
We sit down with you and the people doing the work, map where the hours go, and come back with your top AI opportunities ranked by time and money saved — plus a clear recommendation on what to build first. You decide whether to go further.
Which AI tools do you work with?
We start with what you already have — Microsoft 365 and Copilot, Google Workspace and Gemini, ChatGPT, Power Automate and Power BI. If something else suits you better, we'll recommend it and explain why.
What about data privacy?
We take data privacy and security very seriously. Before we start any project, we sign a Confidentiality Agreement so your data is protected. We also set up AI tools with the right privacy settings and train your team on what should and shouldn't be shared with them.
What if I don't want to give away my data, can you still help?
Absolutely. We can build and test workflows and dashboards using sample data that closely matches your real-world scenarios. Once you're happy with how it works, you switch over to your real data whenever you're ready.
Who is this for?
Small and medium businesses whose teams spend too much time on admin — data entry, reports, emails, approvals and chasing. We've worked with retail, hospitality, e-commerce, trades, professional services and more.
How fast will we see results?
We aim to get your first workflow running in weeks, not months. The assessment gives you a realistic timeline before any work starts.
Do we need Power BI or Microsoft 365?
No. We work with Microsoft 365, Google Workspace or a mix of tools. Power BI is our go-to for dashboards, but we'll fit in with what you already use.
What does it cost?
The 30-minute AI opportunity session is free. After that, you get a clear quote before any work starts, so there are no surprises.
